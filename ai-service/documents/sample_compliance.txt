Section 1: Mango Export Regulations to Singapore

1. Phytosanitary Certificate: All mangoes exported to Singapore must be accompanied by a valid Phytosanitary Certificate issued by the exporting country's National Plant Protection Organization (NPPO).
2. Pests and Diseases: The mangoes must be certified free from fruit flies, particularly the Oriental Fruit Fly (Bactrocera dorsalis). 
3. Post-Harvest Treatment: Mangoes must undergo approved post-harvest treatments such as vapor heat treatment (VHT) or hot water treatment (HWT) to ensure pest eradication.
4. Maximum Residue Limits (MRLs): Pesticide residues on the mangoes must not exceed the limits set by the Singapore Food Agency (SFA).
5. Cold Chain and Storage: Mangoes should be maintained at a temperature between 10°C and 13°C during transport to prevent chilling injury while slowing down the ripening process and spoilage.

---
Section 2: General Packaging Standards

1. Ventilation: Packaging boxes must have adequate ventilation holes to allow air circulation.
2. Labeling: All boxes must be clearly labeled with the crop type, origin, batch ID, and packing date.
3. Materials: Only new, clean, and food-grade packaging materials may be used. Recycled cardboard is permitted if lined with a food-safe barrier.
